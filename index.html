Why Aaroham

Built for families, priced for the long run

Ashiana's curated kid-centric format sits above its standard Kid-Centric Homes line — aimed at dual-income parents who want the community to do some of the raising.

01

A learning hub on campus

Reading hub, music, dance, art & craft, workshop and study rooms. The Live & Learn programme is curated by Hearth Education Advisors and run by a full-time coordinator.

02

Low density, traffic-free greens

6 towers, 542 homes on 10.8 acres, with more than two-thirds of the land green and open. Cars stay below a vehicle-free podium.

03

Built properly

Aluminium formwork, HEPA-filtered fresh air, hot & cold inverter ACs in every room, a fully fitted modular kitchen, and two parking spaces per home.

Phase 3 is expected to launch in December 2026. The Expression of Interest (EOI) window opens on 15 November 2026. EOI holders are typically prioritised for unit selection at launch; EOI amount and terms are as set by the developer.
